# Sources and credits

This independent project takes its name from the Arshinagar song. It is not affiliated with the publishers or editors of Carol Salomon’s *City of Mirrors*, which is separately cited in the research room.

Original Lalon compositions are public domain. Bengali transcriptions taken from Wikisource are reused under [Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), with source links and preserved revision IDs in each retrieved song record. The source edition is *Lalon Gitika*, edited by Motilal Das and Piyushkanti Mahapatra, University of Calcutta, 1958. Edition attribution is not independent authentication. Selected songs from the supplied Mannan edition were transcribed against page images; modern editorial commentary is excluded from the public data.

English renderings are newly prepared, AI-assisted readings of the named Bengali witnesses. They are not Carol Salomon’s or any other scholar’s published translations. Full renderings and excerpts are labeled, and technical uncertainty is identified. The public text dataset is offered under CC BY-SA 4.0 with these credits and provenance retained.

The historic sketch of Lalon is credited to Jyotirindranath Tagore, dated 1889 by the [Wikimedia Commons catalogue](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fakir_Lalon_Shah.jpg), which identifies it as public domain. The courtyard, line illustrations, and record graphics are original abstract artwork for this project, not historical reconstructions or album covers.

Cormorant Garamond, Jost, and Noto Serif Bengali are bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License. Their license files are in `assets/licenses/`. Official performances, label pages, and streaming players retain their respective rights. Video and album players load only on request. No recordings are downloaded or redistributed.

The supplied PDF and modern scholarly texts are retained for local research and excluded from the portable website. A local preview offers a separate loopback route to the supplied PDF; a hosted or portable copy can open a user-selected PDF in the browser without uploading it.
